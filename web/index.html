BulkSend send an NFT or token to hundreds of wallets at once

Testnet only, for now

This works on Robinhood Chain testnet only, so you can try the whole thing with test tokens. Mainnet is switched off until the contract is deployed and tested there. Built from a request in the Robinhood Chain community.

Two ways out, picked for you. If your wallet can send several transfers in one transaction, it sends them as itself: no approval to grant, nothing left to revoke, and it works on collections that only allow transfers their creator approved. Otherwise it uses BulkSend, a contract that holds nothing, takes no fee, and can only move what you approve in the transaction you sign; its source is verified on the explorer.

4 · Check, approve and send

Reviewed by several independent automated audits, not by a human audit firm. After every batch this page asks the chain whether the recipients actually hold what was sent, and records only those. A token that accepts a transfer and moves nothing, or takes a cut on the way, is reported rather than counted. An actively hostile token can lie in its own read functions too, so the explorer is the final word. This page keeps a note, in this browser and for this account only, of what it has already delivered, so reloading the tab will not repeat it. A batch that was signed but never confirmed here is written down too, and read back from the chain next time before anything else is sent. It is a convenience, not a guarantee: another browser, another device, a cleared cache or a private window will not know about it. What has actually been sent is on the chain, and that is the record to trust. To read any Robinhood Chain transaction or contract in plain English before you sign it, including the ones this page builds, use Check. BulkSend holds nothing. Every transfer is transferFrom(you, recipient, …), so it can only move what you approved, inside the transaction you sign.
